Sample sites

Demo mode: the samples are fictional sites with built-in data. Any other address gets a simulated report generated from its name, so the demo never contacts a real website.

    How the real scanner works. The full version is a small PHP app that makes ordinary browser-style requests to a site and checks its response headers, cookies, HTTPS setup, PHP version and a list of files that should never be public. It sends no attack traffic and refuses internal addresses.

    Only scan sites you own or have permission to test.
